Security · BNB Chain · 6–8 Nov

BSC-Reth could compute the wrong state root

BNB Chain released BSC-Reth v0.1.4-hotfix on 6 November to fix “an error that could make Reth calculate the wrong state root under specific conditions.” The fix pulls in an upstream Reth correction to a legacy calculation path. Operators only need to install the release and restart: no database migration or hard fork is required. It applies to mainnet and testnet BSC-Reth nodes; opBNB is not affected (GitHub release, crypto.news). A wrong state root makes a node disagree with the network about the chain's state, so validators running the bug could fork off or produce invalid blocks. This is the main risk of running a newer second client.

BNB Chain also announced the Jenner hard fork for 8 December at 02:30 UTC. It adds a “Payment Lane” that reserves a default 5% of block gas for eligible payment transactions.

UnverifiedWhich versions were affected, and how payments qualify for the Payment Lane. BNB Chain describes its exposure as “limited.”

What to do this week

  • BSC-Reth operators should upgrade to v0.1.4-hotfix now. It only needs a restart.
  • Run a second client in shadow mode and alert on any state-root mismatch with your primary client.
  • Payment apps on BNB Chain should test against Jenner's Payment Lane before 8 December.
  • Add the MEXC designation to your deposit and counterparty screening.

Regulation · United States · 6 Nov

FinCEN names MEXC a money laundering concern

FinCEN has designated the MEXC Global exchange a primary money laundering concern, which bars US financial institutions from moving funds with it. The stated basis includes about $16.7 billion in illicit transfers from 2021 to 2026, sanctions evasion linked to Iran, Russia and North Korea, and more than $26 million tied to the “A7 Network” (US Treasury, CryptoTicker).

UnverifiedThe start date: one source says 30 days after issuance, another 30 days after publication in the Federal Register.

Regulation · Russia · from 17 Nov

Russian retail investors get regulated crypto access

From 17 November, licensed Russian exchanges and brokers may sell BTC, ETH and USDT to retail investors. Non-qualified investors can buy up to 300,000 rubles a year through each intermediary, and the Moscow Exchange is to start on 1 December (CryptoTicker).

UnverifiedI have not read the Bank of Russia's own notice.

BNB Chain · Plain summary

A bug fix for BNB Chain's newer software

Some computers that run BNB Chain use a newer piece of software that could, in rare cases, disagree with the rest of the network about balances. A fix is out and only needs a restart. Ordinary users don't need to do anything.

As a user

  • If you use MEXC and bank with a US institution, plan to move funds before the cut-off takes effect.
  • Check a scanner such as DappBay's Red Alarm list before interacting with unfamiliar BNB Chain contracts.
  • Coincheck users holding GRT, IMX, LSK, MANA, MASK or SAND should withdraw before 17 December.

Coming up: The MEXC effective date, Solana Breakpoint in London (15–17 November), and a new Glamsterdam testnet date.

セキュリティ · BNB Chain · 11月6〜8日

BSC-Reth が誤ったステートルートを計算するおそれ

BNB Chain は11月6日、「特定の条件下で Reth が誤ったステートルートを計算しうる不具合」を修正する BSC-Reth v0.1.4-hotfix を公開しました。上流の Reth で行われた、レガシーな計算経路の修正を取り込んだものです。運用者はリリースを入れて再起動するだけでよく、データベース移行やハードフォークは不要です。対象はメインネットとテストネットの BSC-Reth ノードで、opBNB は影響を受けません（GitHub リリース、crypto.news）。ステートルートが誤るとノードがチェーンの状態についてネットワークと食い違うため、バグのあるバリデータはフォークしたり不正なブロックを作ったりしえます。新しい2つ目のクライアントを動かす際の最大のリスクです。

BNB Chain は、12月8日 02:30 UTC に Jenner ハードフォークを行うことも発表しました。対象となる決済トランザクション向けに、ブロックガスの5%（既定値）を確保する「Payment Lane」を追加します。

未確認影響を受けたバージョンと、Payment Lane の対象となる条件。BNB Chain は影響は「限定的」としています。

今週やること

  • BSC-Reth の運用者は今すぐ v0.1.4-hotfix にアップグレードする。再起動だけで済みます。
  • 2つ目のクライアントをシャドーモードで動かし、主クライアントとのステートルートの不一致を通知する。
  • BNB Chain 上の決済アプリは、12月8日までに Jenner の Payment Lane でテストする。
  • 入金や取引相手のスクリーニングに MEXC の指定を加える。
